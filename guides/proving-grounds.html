Feature Guide

The Proving Grounds

Character Builder & Combat Resolver

Build a 2024 rules character, then take it straight into a fight. The Proving Grounds handles the math, the turns and the bookkeeping so your table can focus on the game. It is free and runs in your browser, with nothing to install and no account to make.

Character Builder

Make a character by the 2024 rules, with every number explained.

Build by the 2024 rules

Choose a class and level, then a species and a background. The builder fills in your Hit Die, saving throws, armor and weapon training, and tells you what each choice gives you. Add a second class with Add a class (multiclass), and it checks the ability score requirements for you.

Customize Origin sits beside your background, so you can choose your own ability score increases, proficiencies and Origin Feat without breaking anything else.

Ability scores

Set your scores with Point Buy, and the builder keeps track of the points you have left. Every modifier shows where it comes from, such as your background or a feat, so you are never left wondering why a number changed.

Spellcasting

Casters get a full spellcasting overview: spellcasting ability, save DC, spell attack bonus, cantrips, prepared spells and spell slots. Wizards keep a spellbook and prepare from it, and every class picks spells from its own list at the levels it can cast.

Inventory and Journal

Track your coins, add mundane and magic items from the lists, or type in your own gear with its own price. Attuned items are counted for you.

The Journal holds the story side of your character: personality traits, ideals, bonds and flaws, faith and alignment, appearance, backstory and session notes.

Further Customization

Most of the time you want the builder to follow the rules. When you don't, for example when your DM hands out an extra feat or a spell from another list, turn on Further Customization. Anything you add or remove beyond the rules is marked in red, so it is always clear what is homebrewed and what is by the book.

A printable character sheet

The Character Sheet tab turns your character into a clean, printable sheet with your abilities, skills, attacks, spells, inventory and features. Print it, save it as a PDF, or download it as a single file that opens in any web browser.

Content Packs: bring your own homebrew

Want a class, subclass, species, spell or item that isn't built in? Content Packs walk you through it in three steps:

  • Download the prompt and give it to your AI assistant along with your homebrew or source book.
  • The AI gives you back a Content Pack file.
  • Upload it, and the new options appear in the builder, ready to use, with their features working in the Combat Resolver.

Packs go into named batches that you can switch on and off with Enable / Disable Content.

Your character stays on your device

There is no login. Your sheet saves itself on your own device under your player code. To move a character to another device, use Save Character File and Load Character File. My Characters keeps everything you have made in one list.

Combat Resolver

Run the fight: initiative, turns, rolls and results, for one screen or the whole table.

Bring your character in

Click Add My Character Sheet and your whole character comes along: ability scores, saving throws, skills, features, spells and equipment. Type your Initiative roll, or let the resolver roll it for you.

341 stat blocks

The Monster Roster holds 341 stat blocks from the System Reference Document. Search by name, filter by creature type and Challenge Rating, and add a monster in one click. Choose whether each one rolls Initiative, uses its average, or lets you type it in.

Run the fight

Press Start Combat and the initiative tracker takes over. Each card is colored by side: yours, allies, hostile, neutral and defeated. Apply damage, healing and Temporary Hit Points straight from the card.

On your turn, your sheet shows what you have left: Action, Bonus Action, Reaction and movement. Click an attack or spell, pick a target, and the resolver rolls it, applies Advantage, Resistance and saving throws, and writes the result to the combat log. Effects with a duration go to Active Effects and count down as the rounds pass.

The dice roller handles any roll you type, plus options like rolling twice and keeping the highest or lowest, or rerolling 1s.

DM Controls

The DM gets a full set of controls to run the table their way:

  • Apply a condition or effect to any creature, with a duration.
  • Set a turn timer for players.
  • Choose how rolls work, such as whether attacks are declared before they are rolled, or whether players can take back their last action.
  • Call for a roll: pick who rolls, the ability or saving throw, and the DC.
  • Switch on table rules like tracking what is in each hand, spell components and ammunition.
  • Decide what players see of your creatures, from exact Hit Points to nothing at all.

Play together online

Start a session and share the invite link. Your players join from their own phones, tablets or computers with their player code, and everyone sees the fight as it happens. The DM approves each new character before it joins the fight.

Sessions are saved, so you can pick up next week where you left off, or start a new session with the same party.

After the Fight

When the DM ends combat, everyone gets the After the Fight panel:

  • Experience: the DM picks which creatures count and hands out Experience Points.
  • What you can do: end Concentration, clear conditions and recover ammunition.
  • Summary: damage dealt and taken, Hit Points and what is still running.
  • Starred log: the moments you starred during the fight, ready to save to your session notes.
